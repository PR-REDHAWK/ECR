# 🚀 EliteX — Powering the Future of Travel

> **ECR (EcoCharge Roommate)**  
> *Power Your Journey Anywhere*

We are **EliteX**, a startup focused on building smart, portable energy solutions for modern travelers.  
Our flagship product, **ECR**, is an all-in-one device designed to provide power, comfort, and convenience in any environment.

---

## 💡 The Problem

Travelers and off-grid users often face:

- 🔋 No reliable power source  
- 🧳 Need to carry multiple devices (fan, lights, chargers)  
- 🌍 Difficulty managing energy in remote areas  

---

## ⚡ Our Solution — ECR

**ECR (EcoCharge Roommate)** is a compact, multi-functional energy device that combines:

- ❄️ Cooling (AC/Fan)  
- 🔥 Heating for cold regions  
- 🔌 Device charging  
- 💡 Built-in lighting  
- ☀️ Solar-powered support  

👉 One device replaces multiple tools.

---

## 🔌 Hardware Overview

ECR is designed as an integrated hardware system:

- 🔋 **Battery Pack** — high-capacity portable energy  
- ☀️ **Solar Module** — off-grid charging  
- ❄️ **Cooling System** — compact AC/fan  
- 🔥 **Heating System** — thermal control  
- 💡 **LED Lighting** — low-power illumination  
- 🔌 **Output Ports** — multi-device charging  

### 🧠 Smart Energy Logic
- Optimizes power usage based on:
  - users, location, duration  
- Ensures efficient energy distribution  
- Designed for real-world travel conditions
